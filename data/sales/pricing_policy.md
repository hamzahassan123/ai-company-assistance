# Pricing Policy - AI Services

## Engagement Models

### 1. Time & Materials (T&M)
- **Hourly Rates:**
  - Partner: $400-500/hour
  - Principal Consultant: $300-350/hour
  - Sr. Data Scientist: $250-300/hour
  - ML Engineer: $200-250/hour
  - Data Engineer: $180-220/hour
  - Jr. Developer: $150-180/hour
- **Best for:** Exploratory work, projects with changing requirements

### 2. Fixed Price
- **Based on:** Detailed scope and requirements
- **Includes:** 10-15% buffer for unknowns
- **Best for:** Well-defined, short-term projects

### 3. Project-Based Milestone
- **Payment Schedule:**
  - 40% upfront
  - 30% at project kickoff
  - 20% at completion of phase 2
  - 10% at final delivery
- **Best for:** Large, multi-phase projects

### 4. Monthly Retainer
- **Retainer Tiers:**
  - Bronze: $10K/month (10 hours support)
  - Silver: $25K/month (20 hours + maintenance)
  - Gold: $50K/month (40 hours + enhancements)
- **Best for:** Ongoing support and maintenance

### 5. Value-Based Pricing
- Pricing tied to business outcomes
- Example: 10% of cost savings or revenue generated
- **Risk:** Complex to measure, client disagreement risk

## Industry-Specific Pricing Factors

| Factor | Impact on Pricing |
|--------|-------------------|
| Data quality | +10-30% if poor/dirty data |
| Infrastructure complexity | +15-25% |
| Security/compliance needs | +20-40% (HIPAA, SOC2) |
| Onsite requirements | +Travel expenses |
| Integration complexity | +20-50% |
| Geographic location | Offshore (30-50% less) vs Onshore |

## Volume Discounts (Multi-Project)

| Project Count | Discount |
|---------------|----------|
| 2-3 projects | 5% off total |
| 4-5 projects | 10% off total |
| 6+ projects | 15% off total |
| Enterprise master agreement | Custom pricing |

## Special Discounts

| Type | Discount | Conditions |
|------|----------|------------|
| Non-profit | 20% | Verification required |
| Government | 25% | Public bidding process |
| First-time client | 10% | New client acquisition |
| Referral | $5K credit | On signed project > $100K |
| Educational institution | 15% | For research collaborations |

## Payment Terms

### Payment Methods
- Wire transfer (preferred)
- ACH/bank transfer
- Credit card (3% processing fee)
- Check (Net 30 days)

### Payment Schedule Guidelines
| Project Size | Standard Schedule |
|--------------|-------------------|
| < $50K | 100% upfront |
| $50-100K | 50% upfront, 50% completion |
| $100-250K | 40/30/20/10 (milestone-based) |
| $250K-1M | 30/20/20/20/10 (milestone-based) |
| > $1M | Custom payment schedule |

### Late Payment Penalties
- 15 days past due: 1.5% monthly interest
- 30 days past due: Work pauses
- 45 days past due: Collections process

## Cost Components

### What's Included in Pricing
- Project management
- AI/ML modeling
- Data preparation
- Training and deployment
- Documentation
- 30-day warranty/post-launch support

### What's Not Included
- Hardware/infrastructure costs
- Third-party software licenses
- Data acquisition costs
- Training beyond included hours
- Travel and expenses (if onsite required)

## IP Ownership

### Standard Terms
- **Client owns:** Deliverables, models, code (paid work)
- **Company retains:** Reusable components, methodology, tools
- **Open source:** All contributions back to OS communities

### Premium Terms (Extra Cost)
- Full IP transfer (including methodology)
- Exclusive rights to solutions
- Patent filing assistance

## Pricing Adjustments

### Annual Rate Review
- Rates reviewed annually (January)
- 5-8% increase typical based on market conditions
- Existing clients locked for project duration

### Change Orders (Scope Creep)
- Must be documented and approved
- Pricing adjusted accordingly
- Formal change order process required
