# Lead Management Process - AI Services

## Lead Sources

### Inbound Leads
- **Content Marketing:** AI thought leadership, whitepapers, webinars
- **Speaking Engagements:** Conferences, industry events
- **Referrals:** Current clients, partners, venture capital firms
- **Digital:** LinkedIn (most effective), SEO for AI services
- **Analyst Firms:** Gartner, Forrester inquiries

### Outbound Leads
- **Strategic Accounts:** Top 100 target companies
- **Partnerships:** Systems integrators, cloud partners (AWS/Azure)
- **Private Equity/Venture:** Portfolios looking to implement AI
- **Events:** Industry conferences (Spark Summit, ODSC, etc.)

## Lead Scoring Model (Service-Oriented)

### Score Components (Total: 100 points)

| Category | Points | Criteria |
|----------|--------|----------|
| **Fit** (50 pts) | | |
| Revenue size | 0-15 | <$10M (5), $10-100M (10), $100M+ (15) |
| Data maturity | 0-20 | No data strategy (5), Some data (10), Advanced (20) |
| AI readiness | 0-15 | None (0), Exploring (5), Implemented (15) |
| **Intent** (50 pts) | | |
| Website behavior | 0-15 | General pages (5), Use cases (10), Services (15) |
| Content engagement | 0-15 | Blog (5), Whitepaper (10), Case study (15) |
| Direct engagement | 0-20 | Event attendance (5), ROI calc (10), Contacted (20) |
| **Urgency** (Bonus) | | |
| Compliance deadline | +10 | GDPR, HIPAA deadline approaching |
| Competitor engagement | +15 | Competition involved |
| CEO-initiated | +20 | Executive-level sponsorship |

### Lead Categories (Service-Oriented)

| Score | Category | Action |
|-------|----------|--------|
| 80-100 | Priority Lead | Immediate engagement, partner call |
| 60-79 | Qualified Lead | Contact within 24 hours |
| 40-59 | Cultivate Lead | Nurture sequence, add to newsletter |
| < 40 | Unqualified | Add to drip campaign |

## Lead Assignment Rules

### Specialization-Based Assignment
- **Industry verticals:** Healthcare, Finance, Retail, etc.
- **Solution type:** NLP, Computer Vision, Tabular Data
- **Project size:** SME vs Enterprise
- **Tech stack:** AWS vs Azure vs GCP expertise

### Capacity Rules (Service Sales)
- Maximum 15 active opportunities per Solutions Architect
- Maximum 8 proposals being drafted simultaneously
- Maximum 5 active negotiations at a time

## Nurturing Sequence (Service-Oriented)

### Cold Lead Nurturing (6-9 Months)
**Month 1-2: Education Phase**
- Content: AI industry trends, educational material
- Webinar invitations
- Case studies in their industry

**Month 3-4: Engagement Phase**
- AI maturity assessment
- Complimentary 1-hour consultation
- ROI calculator for their industry

**Month 5-6: Consideration Phase**
- Custom analysis of one business problem
- Proof of concept proposal
- Reference calls with similar clients

**Month 7-9: Decision Phase**
- Custom proposal
- Executive presentation
- Site visit (if large project)

### Warm Lead Nurturing
- Technical deep-dive sessions
- Free pilot/POC (limited scope)
- Architecture workshop
- Detailed SOW development
- Executive pitch

## Lead Handoff Process

### MQL → SQL Process
1. **Marketing Qualified:** Lead scores > 60
2. **Assessment Call:** 30-min technical scoping
3. **Qualification:** Confirm budget ($100K+ projects)
4. **Sales Qualified:** SOW being drafted
5. **Negotiation:** Legal and commercial terms

### Handoff Checklist
- [ ] Non-disclosure agreement signed
- [ ] Budget confirmed ($100K+)
- [ ] Decision-maker identified
- [ ] Timeline (6-12 months)
- [ ] Scope initial understanding
- [ ] Internal champion identified
- [ ] Data availability assessed

## CRM Hygiene (Service-Specific)

### Required Fields
- **Company:** Revenue, industry, employee count, tech stack
- **Contact:** Job title, influence level, technical comfort
- **Project:** Budget range, timeline, scope, decision process
- **Competition:** Known competitors, status
- **Internal:** Solution Architect assigned, legal review status

### Monthly Tasks
- Review all active opportunities
- Update stage progression
- Check for stagnation (60+ days no activity)
- Competitor status updates
