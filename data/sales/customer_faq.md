## Implementation Process

### 8. What's your implementation methodology?
We use an **Agile + MLOps** approach:
1. **Discovery:** Understand business problem
2. **Data Assessment:** Evaluate data readiness
3. **Prototype:** Quick model iteration
4. **Development:** Build production solution
5. **Deployment:** CI/CD + Model monitoring
6. **Handoff:** Training and knowledge transfer
