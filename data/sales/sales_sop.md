# Sales Standard Operating Procedures (SOP) - AI Services

## Overview
This document outlines the standard procedures for selling AI consulting and implementation services.

## 1. Lead Qualification Process

### 1.1 Initial Contact
- Respond to all inbound leads within 2 business hours
- Use the **CHAMP** framework for qualification:
  - **C**hallenges: What business problems need solving?
  - **H**ealth: What's the urgency? (Compliance, revenue loss, etc.)
  - **A**uthority: Who makes the final decision?
  - **M**oney: What's the budget and approval process?
  - **P**rioritization: Where does this rank among their initiatives?

### 1.2 Discovery Call
- Duration: 45-60 minutes (more complex than SaaS)
- Key questions to cover:
  1. What specific business challenge are you trying to solve?
  2. Have you attempted AI solutions before? What happened?
  3. What's your data maturity level?
  4. Who are the internal champions/stakeholders?
  5. What's your timeline and budget?
  6. Do you have in-house AI/ML capabilities?

### 1.3 Technical Assessment (Critical for Services)
- Schedule within 5-7 business days
- Activities:
  - Data audit and assessment
  - Infrastructure review
  - Current workflow analysis
  - Feasibility study
  - Identify risks and dependencies

### 1.4 Proposal Presentation
- Custom solution design
- Timeline: 3-6 months typical
- Team composition
- Statement of Work (SOW)
- Pricing (project-based)

## 2. Project Scoping and Pricing

### 2.1 Types of Engagements
| Type | Duration | Price Range | When to Offer |
|------|----------|-------------|---------------|
| **AI Discovery** | 2-4 weeks | $15K-$30K | Initial exploration |
| **Proof of Concept** | 4-8 weeks | $50K-$100K | Before full commitment |
| **MVP Development** | 3-6 months | $100K-$250K | First production system |
| **Full Implementation** | 6-12 months | $250K-$1M+ | Enterprise transformation |
| **Ongoing Support** | Monthly retainer | $10K-$50K/month | Mature systems |
